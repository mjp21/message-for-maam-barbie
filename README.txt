BACKGROUND MUSIC:
- Put your chosen audio file in this same project folder (beside index.html).
- Rename the audio file to exactly: music.mp3
- Click the floating "Play music" button at the bottom-right of the website.
- Click "Pause music" to pause it. The track loops while playing.
- Music does not autoplay; visitors need to press Play because browsers restrict autoplay.


AUTOMATIC MUSIC WHEN THE LETTER OPENS:
- The audio path is set to MUSIC/BG.mp3, matching the folder and filename in your VS Code screenshot.
- When the visitor clicks or presses Enter/Space on the envelope to open the letter, the music starts.
- The floating music button still lets the visitor pause or play the music manually.
- Some browsers may still block playback due to their autoplay policy; starting music from the envelope click is the reliable approach. If blocked, the visitor can tap the Play music button.
